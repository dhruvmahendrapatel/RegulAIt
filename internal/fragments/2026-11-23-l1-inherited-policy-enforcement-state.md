**2026-11-23 - Inherited policy enforced at call time ([ADR-0357](../decisions/0357-governance-config-as-code-and-policy-inheritance.md) "Enforcement at call time", lane L1, owner decision D-2026-11-23-02; pending migration `system_reason_governance_policy_read`).** The governed decision core now applies the effective inherited policy (org → business unit → project) on every model, connector, MCP and A2A call. `allowedAgents`, `allowedConnectors`, `allowedMcpServers` and `deniedTools` refuse outside the policy (deny wins), and each applied rule is audited with the level that supplied it. A policy that cannot be resolved refuses the call. The other inherited rules still report `not_enforced`.
