**2026-11-17 - lane E32 slice 2: the EU AI Act deployer-obligation register ([ADR-0351](../decisions/0351-evidence-extras-syslog-deployer-register-endpoint-benchmark.md) §2, no migration).** A new pack, `eu-ai-act-deployer@1`, maps Art. 26(1)-(12) plus Art. 4, 27, 49(3), 50(3)/(4), 73 and 86. Five duties are observed (real collectors, with a link to the evidence) and thirteen are attested (who, when, evidence reference, review date). `GET /v1/compliance/deployer-register` is a typed view over the active pack, and `POST .../attest` follows one declared lifecycle table (`open`, `observed`, `attested`, `review_due`). An observed duty cannot be attested, and every attestation needs a review date at most 366 days ahead. Pack attestations now have one write path (`recordPackAttestation`). API only; UI on #453.
