**2026-11-21 - P-01c item 1, slice 4c: ABAC holds queued on model and connector calls ([ADR-0251](../decisions/0251-one-governed-decision-core.md) "Slice 4c", lane L1; no migration).** An ABAC v5 `require_approval` on a model dispatch or connector call now holds it in the approvals queue for the policy's approver, instead of refusing. The consent is its own binding, and its context digest carries the policy version, so a new version makes it stale and no rule's approver can release it. A2A still refuses (403 `approval_required`; it has no approval queue).
