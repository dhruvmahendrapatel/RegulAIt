## Incidents and fixes
- **Lock PRs never opened since 2026-10-18.** The `lock-pr` job of `engine-images.yml` failed at `gh pr create` on
  every publish, because the repository setting that lets Actions create PRs is off. The bot branches were pushed,
  but no PR was opened. After #471 merged, the lock still named garak 0.17.0, so the garak engine stayed fail-closed
  on main from 11:09 until the master opened #479 from the bot's branch and it merged at 13:26. #480 made the job
  push-only (owner decision).
- **A workflow-only edit republished every engine image.** The #480 merge rebuilt all three images (#483). The #483
  review raised it (Medium) and found the builds were not reproducible (Low). The owner chose the publish gate and
  reproducible builds (#485). The #485 merge's run was the first reproducible publish; its lock PR is #487.
- **Missed publish.** The #481 merge also triggered a publish (before the gate was on main), and the master did not
  open its lock PR. That branch (`engine-images-lock/1212611a575e`) was superseded by the #485 publish. Lesson: check
  the engine image runs after every merge, not only after engine-looking PRs.
- **X16 literacy flake** (`adr0182-a14-literacy.mock.spec.ts`, `Save key` timeout): seen on #465, passed on re-run.
  #481 hardened the spec, but the review found the cause not reproduced, so it is not fixed.
- **Router size estimate.** The "about 32.8 kB" saving from the routing library's production build was the size of
  the whole library. The real saving is about 0.6 kB (#484 review), which changed the ceiling decision at 18:16.
- **#477 ceiling-exception guard exploit.** The #477 recheck (M3) reproduced that the guard accepted any exception
  row citing the recorded decision link. Fixed in round 3 (one row allows exactly one raise, from and to stated
  values) and approved on delta recheck.
- **#477 over the bundle ceilings.** Combined with #474, #477 was 585 B over the entry ceiling (review H1). Moving
  the fold code to a lazy chunk still left both ceilings exceeded, so the fixer was told not to raise them and the
  owner decided (15:49).
- **Restarts.** A container restart at 03:19, and merge-loop timer ends at about 05:15 and 12:07 (restarted 08:12 and
  12:42, while owner questions were open) plus restarts at 16:37 and 18:35. No merge was lost.
