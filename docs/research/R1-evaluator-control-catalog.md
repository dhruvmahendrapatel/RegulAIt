Scope: 35 original evaluation proposals, not shipped detectors or certified controls. Each requires fixtures, an owner-approved threshold, failure/unknown outcomes and versioned results; probabilistic judges require labelled holdouts and human review. A passed test evidences only its tested property.

The suite's `docs/contracts/control-identifiers.md` forbids new unregistered control identifiers. NIST subcategory and ISO subcontrol mappings are therefore explicitly UNVERIFIED, rather than copying the previous unsupported pairings. This document does not amend the suite contract. The source establishes framework context, not endorsement of these proposed tests. ISO's [publisher page](https://www.iso.org/standard/42001) confirms the standard; it does not expose sufficient normative Annex A text to complete the subcontrol crosswalk (checked 2026-10-04). Suite-owner registration and normative review are required before automatic mapping or public compliance claims.

OWASP column is pinned to the requested **2025** risk namespace, not a claim that 2025 is the latest edition. Risk-category relevance is an editorial proposal, not a control equivalence. EU articles use existing suite-registered identifiers; no applicability dates or universal obligations are inferred. Image/audio disclosure requirements are left unmapped rather than inventing a universal watermark rule.
